FAQ⌄

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: [Windows] What to do if “Unauthorized changes blocked” notification appears?

A: The apps needs access to the folder they are trying to modify. Go to Settings > Allowed Apps > Add an allowed app > Optimizer Apps.exe

Q: [MacOS] What to do if "blocked from use because it is not from an identified developer" notification appears?

A: The apps needs your approval. Go to System Preferences > Security & Privacy > Security > Open Anyway

Q: [MacOS] Why is there no Images Optimizer for the Mac?

A: Images Optimizer is available on Windows and Linux. A Mac version is coming soon. In the meantime, enjoy Videos Optimizer.

Q: [Linux] Why is there no Videos Optimizer for Linux?

A: Videos Optimizer is available on Windows and Mac. A Linux version is coming soon. In the meantime, enjoy Images Optimizer.
